# Design

Sales surfaces under `/{locale}/agents` are a product world, not the studio.

## Light

Paper field `#F4F3EF`, ink `#121212`, mute `#4A4742` / `#5C5954`. Vermillion `#E23B2E` is the buy action only.

## Chrome

Wordmark Karukera + Agents links to `/agents`. No Studio, no home. Product links: Audit sécu, Secrétaire. Language. Compact price on desktop.

## Mechanism

The first viewport is the squad console: twelve agents, a live journal, a finding that appears when Authz fires. Labeled demo. Dark console is a stage, not the page chrome.

## Features

Four staged product UIs (code, outside, inside, tickets). CSS, not stock photos. Alternating text / stage like a product landing, not a card grid.

## Type

Space Grotesk for sales. Shippori only on the wordmark. Headlines wrap; no kickers.
